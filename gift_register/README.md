# GiftRegister

Business Gift and Hospitality Log

## The problem

Business gifts and hospitality tracked informally lack a consistent, auditable record and approval trail.

## The solution

Record business gifts given or received with counterpart, value and purpose, track declaration and approval states, and keep a company-safe compliance register.

## Main features

- Gift records for gifts given or received.
- Counterpart, date, value, purpose and responsible fields.
- Draft, declared, approved and refused states.
- Approval and refusal actions.
- Search and group by direction, employee and state.
- Chatter tracking on records.
- Multi-company safe, no Enterprise dependency.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Gift Register** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 20.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 18.0 and 19.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
