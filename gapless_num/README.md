# GaplessNum

Legal Numbering Without Gaps

## The problem

A gap in a legal invoice sequence is a question on every audit, and Odoo lets anyone rewrite the number of a posted entry without leaving a trace. The company discovers the gap months later and cannot say who changed the number or when.

## The solution

Detect, trace and block gaps, duplicates and manual renumbering in legal document sequences, with a dated audit journal per journal and company.

## Main features

- Detect missing numbers in any journal and any period.
- Detect duplicate and out-of-order legal numbers.
- Warn or block a manual change of a posted number.
- Dated audit journal linked to the move and the journal.
- Resolve, ignore and reopen anomalies.
- Idempotent scan that never duplicates an anomaly.
- Kanban, graph and pivot analysis.
- Multi-company isolation out of the box.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Numbering** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 20.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 18.0 and 19.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
