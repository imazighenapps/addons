# DeferralPro

Prepaid Expenses and Deferred Revenue

## The problem

A twelve-month premium, an annual support contract or a yearly invoice is booked in one month and then split again by hand in a spreadsheet. One forgotten period and the monthly result is wrong, the prepaid balance no longer matches the ledger, and nobody can prove which month was already expensed.

## The solution

Spread prepaid expenses and deferred revenue over their real period, post one month or the whole schedule, and keep posted and remaining amounts exact.

## Main features

- Prepaid expense and deferred revenue schedules.
- Monthly lines generated from the covered period.
- Rounded lines whose total equals the deferred amount.
- Post one period at a time or the whole schedule.
- Automatic balanced journal entry from a journal and two accounts.
- Posted and remaining amounts recomputed after each posting.
- Draft, running, done and cancelled states with an audit trail.
- Multi-company and multi-currency, no Enterprise dependency.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Deferrals** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 19.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 18.0 and 20.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
