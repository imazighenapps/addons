# RetainerBook

Hours Visible, Always

## The problem

Retainer hour banks are tracked in spreadsheets, so remaining balances and burn-down are invisible to clients and teams until the bank is already empty.

## The solution

Sell hours in advance as retainers and show each customer how their bank burns down contract after contract.

## Main features

- A retainer bank per customer with purchased hours and validity dates.
- Usage entries that log the hours consumed over time.
- Remaining hours and burn-down percentage computed and stored.
- A low-balance threshold to flag banks running out of hours.
- Close and reopen actions to freeze or reactivate a bank.
- Usage above the remaining balance is refused for you.
- Customer-linked banks with a clear burn-down history.
- Multi-company isolation out of the box.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **RetainerBook** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 20.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 18.0 and 19.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
