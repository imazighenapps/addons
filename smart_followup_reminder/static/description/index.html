The #1 reason deals go cold?

A salesperson sends a quote and… forgets to follow up. The prospect moves on. Research shows that 80% of sales require 5+ follow-ups, yet most reps give up after just one. Smart Follow-up Reminder automates this entire process inside your existing Odoo Sales workflow.

Everything your team needs to follow up faster

Six powerful features, zero extra tools required.

⚙️
Per-Team Configuration

Define custom delay thresholds for each sales team — 1st reminder, 2nd reminder, and manager escalation. Set a minimum order amount to avoid reminders on small deals.

🤖
Daily Automation at 8 AM

A scheduled cron runs every morning and automatically flags overdue quotes. It creates Odoo mail.activity tasks for salespeople and sends internal notifications for escalated quotes.

📧
1-Click Follow-up Wizard

Hit "Send Follow-up" on any overdue quotation to open a pre-filled email wizard. The subject and body are automatically rendered from your custom email template. Send in seconds.

📊
Color-Coded Status Badges

Every quotation list now shows a follow-up status badge: OK, Pending, Overdue, or Escalated. The status bar is also visible on the form view.

🚨
Manager Escalation

When a quote reaches the escalation threshold, the sales team manager automatically receives an Odoo internal notification with the quote name, customer, and days without reply.

📋
Kanban Board

A dedicated Kanban view groups all quotes to follow up by status. Each card shows the customer name, deal amount, days without reply (red badge if >7 days), and a quick action follow-up button.

Up and running in 3 steps

1
Configure

Go to Sales › Follow-ups › Configuration and create one rule per sales team. Set your delay thresholds and choose your email template.

2
Monitor

The daily cron runs automatically at 8 AM. Salespeople see activities appear in their inbox. Navigate to Sales › Follow-ups › Quotes to Follow Up for the full pipeline view.

3
Act

Click Send Follow-up on any overdue quote. The wizard pre-fills everything. One click to send — follow-up count and date are automatically recorded.

Technical highlights

  • ✓ Computed fields days_without_reply and followup_status stored on sale.order
  • ✓ Inbound reply detection via mail.message author check
  • ✓ mail.activity auto-created — no duplicate activities per order
  • ✓ Cron frequency and time fully adjustable in Settings › Technical
  • ✓ Security: salesman can read/write their team config, manager has full access
  • ✓ All strings translated with _() — fully i18n-ready
