# InterCo Flow

Automated Intercompany Trading

## The problem

In a group, the second leg of an intercompany sale is retyped by hand: lines are forgotten, the transfer price is wrong, and the group margin vanishes in a consolidation nobody can explain.

## The solution

Turn a confirmed sales order into the matching purchase order in another company of the group, with product mapping, transfer margin and a traced link on both sides.

## Main features

- One rule per selling company and target company.
- Price policy: same price, margin or standard cost.
- Product mapping between the two catalogues.
- Transfer record with sale, purchase and margin amounts.
- Purchase order created in the target company in one click.
- Link and origin traced on both sides.
- Cancel workflow, one transfer per sales order.
- Multi-company isolation out of the box.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Intercompany** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 18.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 19.0 and 20.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
