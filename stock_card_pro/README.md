# StockCard Pro - Valued Stock Card

## The problem

When the auditor asks for the stock card of one product over one quarter, teams export three Excel files and still cannot reconcile the opening quantity.

## The solution

Audit-ready stock card per product, warehouse and lot: opening, inbound, outbound, closing quantity and value, with PDF.

## Main features

- Stock card per product, warehouse and lot/serial.
- Opening quantity rebuilt from history.
- Inbound and outbound lines with references.
- Closing quantity and closing value.
- Professional PDF.
- Multi-company.
- Traceability to every stock move.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Stock Cards** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 18.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 19.0 and 20.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
