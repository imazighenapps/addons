# EarlyPay

Discounts Never Missed

## The problem

Early payment discounts are buried in supplier terms, so the window closes unnoticed and the saving is lost with no record of what was offered or missed.

## The solution

Grab every early payment discount vendors offer: the due window, the gain per booking and what you let slip.

## Main features

- A register of vendor early payment discount offers.
- Gross amount, discount rate and stored discount amount per offer.
- Discount deadline and normal due date on every offer.
- Stored net amount to pay when the discount is captured.
- Open, captured and missed states with a dated decision.
- One screen for offers whose discount window is still open.
- Impossible dates and rates refused before they are saved.
- Multi-company isolation out of the box.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Early Pay** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 20.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 18.0 and 19.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
