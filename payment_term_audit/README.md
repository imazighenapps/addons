# TermAudit

Payment Terms That Match

## The problem

A customer signs thirty-day terms, then invoices ship with a mismatched due date or no payment term at all, and the gap is only discovered when the money is late.

## The solution

Compare the payment terms stored on each customer with the due dates actually written on the invoices, and list every gap before it costs a late payment.

## Main features

- One dated audit per period.
- Expected days from the partner payment term.
- Actual days from the invoice due date.
- Configurable tolerance in days.
- Missing terms as their own anomaly.
- Ignore or reopen any anomaly.
- Replayable without duplicates.
- Company isolation and kind filters.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Payment Term Audits** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 20.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 18.0 and 19.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
