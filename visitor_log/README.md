# VisitorLog

Front Desk Register

## The problem

The paper register at the front desk answers no question: nobody can prove who entered the building, which employee hosted the visitor, or how long a technician stayed on site. The data is unreadable, never archived and impossible to purge when the retention policy says so.

## The solution

Sign visitors in and out at the front desk, record who hosts them, and purge the register automatically after the retention period your policy allows.

## Main features

- Front desk register with visitor, company, phone and email.
- Host per visit, with a grouped view by employee.
- Purpose of the visit, from meeting to maintenance.
- Badge number, check-in and check-out timestamps.
- Duration on site computed and stored on every visit.
- Expected, on site, checked out and cancelled states.
- Purge wizard by check-out date, current company only.
- Multi-company isolation out of the box.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Visitor Log** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 19.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 18.0 and 20.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
