QMS Management System
======================

Overview
--------

QMS Management System provides a modular quality-management workspace for requirements, evidence, audits, NCR/CAPA, controlled documents, competence, supplier quality, complaints, measurement management and management review.

Key workflows
-------------

* Requirement to evidence traceability
* Risk and control management
* Audit programme, audit checks and findings
* NCR, root-cause analysis, CAPA and effectiveness verification
* Controlled document revisions and acknowledgements
* Training and competency records
* Customer complaints and SLA tracking
* Measurement equipment and calibration history
* Supplier qualification and certification lifecycle
* Standard edition transitions and requirement mappings
* Executive readiness indicators and compliance matrix

Standards
--------

The data model is generic and can support multiple management-system standards. Do not distribute or embed copyrighted standard text without the required license. Load appropriately licensed requirements or customer-owned requirements into the QMS.

Languages
---------

English is the source language. UI translation packs are supplied for French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese and German.
