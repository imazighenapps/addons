# MedVisit

Visits Never Overdue

## The problem

Occupational medical visit deadlines are tracked in calendars and spreadsheets, so overdue visits are discovered only during audits or incidents.

## The solution

Track the occupational medical visits of every employee and never discover an overdue one at the worst time.

## Main features

- A medical visit per employee with type, schedule and due date.
- A stored overdue indicator computed from the due date.
- Completion and cancellation actions with a traced status.
- The visit cannot be completed without a completion date.
- A due date before the scheduled date is refused for you.
- Search filters to surface overdue and upcoming visits quickly.
- Employee-linked visits for HR follow-up.
- Multi-company isolation out of the box.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **MedVisit** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 18.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 19.0 and 20.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
