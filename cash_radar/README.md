# CashRadar

13-Week Cash Forecast

## The problem

A cash hole is discovered when it arrives, not when it is decided. Spreadsheets are stale by the first week, they ignore the due dates of the open invoices, and nobody replays the scenario when the biggest customer announces a delay.

## The solution

Project thirteen weeks of cash from open invoices, vendor bills and recurring entries, with a delay scenario, weekly net flow and the exact week the balance turns negative.

## Main features

- Rolling forecast of thirteen weeks, configurable.
- Open customer invoices and vendor bills by due date.
- Recurring inflows and outflows, weekly to quarterly.
- Delay scenario for late-paying customers.
- Weekly net flow and running balance.
- Lowest point and the week the balance turns negative.
- Several scenarios with different opening balances.
- Graph and pivot by week, multi-company isolation.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Cash Flow** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 19.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 18.0 and 20.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
