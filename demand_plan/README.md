# ForecastBook

Commit, Then Compare

## The problem

Sales forecasts are kept in personal spreadsheets, so commitments are never compared with real sales and the team cannot learn from the gap month after month.

## The solution

Let each salesperson commit a monthly forecast per product and compare it with real sales, month after month.

## Main features

- Monthly forecast plans per salesperson with a clear period.
- Forecast lines per product with committed quantity and revenue.
- Actual quantity and revenue recorded next to every commitment.
- Variance quantity and revenue computed and stored per line.
- A simple workflow from draft to committed to closed.
- Plan totals computed from the forecast lines.
- Closing a plan without lines is refused for you.
- Multi-company isolation out of the box.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Forecasts** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 19.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 18.0 and 20.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
