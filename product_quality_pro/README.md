# ProductQuality

Clean Product Master Data

## The problem

Product sheets are created in a hurry: no barcode, a zero weight, a cost left at zero, an empty sales description. Purchasing, sales and the warehouse each discover the hole at their own expense, and nobody owns a global picture of what is missing.

## The solution

Score every product sheet against your own completeness rules, list the missing barcodes, weights, costs and categories, and watch the score improve scan after scan.

## Main features

- Your own completeness rules, one per field.
- A severity and a weight in points per rule.
- A scan checks every active product of the company.
- One anomaly line per missing field, with a message.
- A 0-100 score weighted by the rule points.
- Idempotent re-runs, no duplicate anomaly.
- Anomalies marked as new, fixed or ignored.
- Graph and pivot on the score, multi-company isolation.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Product Quality** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 18.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 19.0 and 20.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
