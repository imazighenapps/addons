# PriceGuard - Purchase Price Compliance

## The problem

Suppliers quietly increase their prices and the accounting team pays the bill without comparing it to the negotiated agreement. The overprice is discovered at the end of the year, if ever.

## The solution

Control supplier price agreements and flag vendor bills whose price exceeds the agreed price or tolerance.

## Main features

- Price agreement per supplier, product and period.
- Minimum quantity and validity dates.
- Configurable tolerance percentage.
- Automatic scan of posted vendor bills.
- Warning and breach levels.
- No duplicate check when scanning again.
- Ignore a check with a documented decision.
- Grouped views per supplier and status.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Price Guard** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 19.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 18.0 and 20.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
