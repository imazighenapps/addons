Feature 01

Real-Time Queue Board

Your staff gets a live kanban view of every visitor in the waiting area. See who is Waiting, Called, or In Service — updated instantly, no refresh needed.

  • ✓ Add visitors manually or via kiosk self check-in
  • ✓ One-click Call, Start Service, Done, No Show actions
  • ✓ Re-call button for unanswered announcements
  • ✓ Overdue alerts for visitors waiting too long
  • ✓ Multi-room and multi-department support
Feature 02

Stunning TV Display Screen

A public URL requires zero login. Open it on any TV or screen in your waiting area. Patients can see exactly who is being served and what position they are in the queue.

  • ✓ "Now Serving" token shown in large, clear display
  • ✓ Waiting list with position, name, service & estimated wait
  • ✓ Spectacular animated call overlay when a patient is called
  • ✓ Audio voice announcement (browser Text-to-Speech)
  • ✓ Configurable scrolling ticker message at the bottom
  • ✓ Zero-flicker DOM diffing — no visible refresh
Feature 03

Self Check-in Kiosk

Reduce workload at reception. Visitors check themselves in on a tablet and receive their token number instantly — no staff intervention needed. Optimized for touchscreens.

  • ✓ Intuitive 3-step flow: tap → select service → enter name
  • ✓ Shows token number, queue position & estimated wait
  • ✓ Auto-resets after 22s (inactivity reset after 90s)
  • ✓ No login required — open on any tablet
  • ✓ Works offline after initial page load
Feature 04

Smart Priority System

Not all visitors are equal. Serve the right person at the right time with a 4-level intelligent prioritization engine.

—
Normal
Default for all walk-in visitors. Served in order of arrival.
↑
Priority
For appointment holders, elderly patients, or special cases.
⚡
Urgent
Emergencies and acute pain. Moves ahead of all Priority visitors.
★
VIP
Maximum priority. Served immediately after the current patient.
Feature 05

Visit History & Daily Reports

Every visit is permanently logged. Management gets full visibility into wait times, no-show rates, and service efficiency — all exportable to a printable PDF report.

  • ✓ Complete history: Done, No Show, Cancelled
  • ✓ Wait time & service duration per visit
  • ✓ Satisfaction ratings (1–5 stars) & visitor feedback
  • ✓ Printable PDF daily report from any room
  • ✓ Filter by date, room, department, status
