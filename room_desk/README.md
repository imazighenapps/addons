# RoomBook

No Double Booking, Ever

## The problem

Meeting rooms are booked in chats and side calendars, two teams show up at the same time, and nobody can prove who had the room or why the projector was not there.

## The solution

Book the meeting rooms without the double-booking drama, with capacity, equipment and a clean conflict rule.

## Main features

- A register of meeting rooms with location, capacity and equipment.
- Bookings with host, subject, attendees and exact start and end.
- A conflict rule that refuses overlapping bookings in the same room.
- Draft, confirmed and cancelled states with a full chatter trail.
- Room and upcoming booking counters kept up to date on each room.
- A clean agenda view grouped by room and by day.
- Multi-company isolation out of the box.
- No calendar integration required: the room book is the source of truth.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Rooms** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 19.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 18.0 and 20.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
