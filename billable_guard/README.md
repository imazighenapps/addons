# BillableGuard

Unbilled Time Alerts

## The problem

In an agency, the first loss is not a bad rate: it is time that was worked, logged, and never invoiced. Nobody sees it because timesheets and invoices live in two different screens and the quarter closes before anyone reconciles them.

## The solution

Compare logged timesheets with what has actually been invoiced, flag every unbilled hour with its amount at risk and prepare the invoice in one click.

## Main features

- Detect unbilled hours from real timesheets.
- Compare hours logged and hours invoiced per project.
- Compute the amount at risk at the sales order price.
- Minimum-hours threshold per scan.
- Low, medium and high risk on the project.
- One click to open the invoice wizard.
- Ignore and reopen workflow with a chatter trail.
- Kanban, graph and pivot, multi-company isolation.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Billing** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 18.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 19.0 and 20.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
