Contracts shouldn't live in a folder nobody opens

A missed renewal date. A discount nobody approved. A signed PDF buried in someone's inbox. Without a system of record, every contract is a small risk waiting to become a big one. Smart Contract Lifecycle turns your contracts into structured, trackable records — fully integrated with the Sales, Purchase and Accounting apps you already use.

📄

All Contract Types, One App

Customer, vendor, partnership, NDA, employment, lease, service or custom — manage every contract family with one unified, versioned record and a full change history.

✅

Amount-Based Approval Matrix

Configure approval brackets by contract value, each with its own sequence of approval levels and security groups. High-value contracts are automatically routed to the right people — no more "who signs off on this?" Slack threads.

⚠️

Automatic Risk Score

Every contract gets a 0–100 risk score, computed automatically from missing termination clauses, high amounts without recorded approval, stalled negotiations, overdue milestones and partner history. Know what needs attention before it becomes a problem.

🔔

Expiry, Renewal & Milestone Alerts

A daily automated check flags contracts nearing their notice period, expires contracts past their end date, and schedules activities and email reminders — automatically, with zero manual follow-up.

🔁

Manual or Automatic Renewal

Renew with one click via a guided wizard, or let recurring contracts renew themselves on their end date. Each renewal is created as a new linked version, keeping full lineage back to the original agreement.

💶

Recurring Billing & Invoicing

Define billing lines per contract — monthly, quarterly, semi-annual or annual — and let the app generate invoices automatically through Odoo Accounting. Track invoiced vs. remaining amount on every contract, in real time.

✍️

Signature Tracking

Record signature status for your company and the partner, attach signature images, and get a clean, professional signed PDF — generated directly from the contract record.

📚

Reusable Legal Clause Library

Build a company-wide library of clauses — confidentiality, termination, penalties, liability, IP, GDPR, payment, force majeure, jurisdiction — and insert them into any contract in one click via the clause wizard.

📝

Dynamic Contract Templates

Start from a template with placeholder variables — partner name, dates, amount, company, reference — automatically filled in when applied to a new contract. Standardize wording without re-typing it every time.

🔗

Native Sales & Purchase Integration

Link existing quotations and vendor orders to a contract, or create new ones directly from it. Contracts stay connected to the commercial documents that generated them.

📑

Amendments & Milestones

Track formal amendments to active contracts and manage deliverables or milestones with due dates and status — so deadlines are visible long before they're missed.

🌐

Customer Portal Access

Your customers and vendors can view their own contracts and download the signed PDF from their Odoo portal — no extra logins, no emailing documents back and forth.

Financial Dashboard

See your contract portfolio the way your CFO does

One screen brings together Monthly & Annual Recurring Revenue, renewal rate, contract distribution by type and status, and a risk-versus-value map of your entire portfolio — so you spend less time exporting data and more time acting on it.
