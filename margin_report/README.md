# MarginBook

Margins Without Guesswork

## The problem

The realized margin of a confirmed order is reconstructed after the fact from scattered numbers: revenue lives in the sale, cost lives elsewhere, and the meeting argues with opinions instead of reading a frozen, line-by-line margin.

## The solution

Show the realized margin of every confirmed order and invoice, line by line, without leaving the sale.

## Main features

- One margin sheet per order with customer and salesperson.
- Lines with quantity, selling price and unit cost incurred.
- Revenue, cost, net margin and margin rate computed live.
- A target margin per sheet and an under-target flag.
- Confirming freezes the lines of the sheet.
- The detail screen breaks the margin down line by line.
- The low-margin screen lists the lines that lose money.
- Multi-company isolation out of the box.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Margin Book** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 19.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 18.0 and 20.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
