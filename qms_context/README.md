# QMS Context & Compliance

## Purpose
QMS Context & Compliance captures the organization's context, interested parties, management-system scope, compliance obligations, climate relevance and improvement opportunities.

## Main capabilities
### Context issues
- Record internal and external issues.
- Categorize issues across strategic, customer, technology, people, regulatory, supply chain, environment, health & safety, financial or other topics.
- Record impact, owner and review date.
- Assess climate relevance as not assessed, not relevant, relevant or material.
- Approve or supersede context records.

### Interested parties
- Record customers, employees/workers, suppliers, regulators, owners/investors, communities and business partners.
- Capture needs and expectations.
- Capture related requirements and monitoring methods.
- Record relevance, ownership and review dates.

### Management-system scope
- Define scope text, sites, products/services and processes.
- Document exclusions.
- Track effective and review dates.
- Assess climate relevance.
- Approve or supersede scope statements.

### Compliance obligations
- Register legal/regulatory, contractual, customer, industry/voluntary and internal commitments.
- Store source reference and description.
- Link obligations to applicable processes and evidence.
- Track review dates and status from identified through compliance/gap identified.
- Validate date consistency.

### Opportunities
- Register opportunities from audits, risks, complaints, objectives, employee suggestions or management review.
- Set priority, owner, target date and action plan.
- Track from identified through approved, in-progress, completed or rejected.
- Require a result when completing an opportunity.
