# CommissionDesk — Sales Commission Engine

## The problem

Sales commissions are still calculated in spreadsheets in most SMEs. The result is predictable: two to five days of work every month, calculation errors, disputes at payroll, commissions paid on unpaid invoices, and no clawback when a customer returns a product.

## The solution

CommissionDesk turns Odoo sales orders, invoices and payments into clear, auditable commission lines. Define the rules once, generate a monthly statement in one click, validate it, and pay the right amount every time.

## Main features

- Commission rules by salesperson, sales team, customer, product category or margin.
- Three calculation bases: confirmed order, posted invoice, registered payment.
- Percentage or fixed amount, with volume tiers.
- Monthly statement per salesperson, ready for PDF and portal.
- Validation workflow: sales manager, then finance.
- Automatic clawback on credit notes and unpaid invoices.
- Multi-currency and multi-company.
- Full audit trail and CSV / SEPA export.

## Who is it for?

Distribution, wholesale, agencies and service companies with 5 to 30 salespeople, using Odoo Community or Enterprise.

## Installation

1. Install the module.
2. Go to **Commissions > Rules** and create your first rule.
3. Go to **Commissions > Statements**, select a salesperson and a period.
4. Click **Generate Lines**, review, then **Submit**, **Approve** and **Mark as Paid**.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 20.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 18.0 and 19.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external dependency.
