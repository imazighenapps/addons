# OnTime

Promised, Then Delivered

## The problem

The delivery date promised on every order confirmation is never compared with what shipped: disputes are settled by opinion, the late orders are reargued one by one and the on-time rate stays a guess.

## The solution

Compare the delivery date promised on each order with what the warehouse really shipped and score your on-time rate.

## Main features

- One delivery commitment per order, with its promise date.
- The real shipment date recorded in one click.
- The delay in days, computed and stored on each shipment.
- Early and on-time deliveries both scored as on time.
- One late screen of the orders that missed the promise.
- One on-time screen to score the promise book as a whole.
- A shipment without its date is refused for you.
- Multi-company isolation out of the box.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Delivery Watch** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 20.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 18.0 and 19.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
