# ParcelWatch

Tracking At One Click

## The problem

Tracking numbers live in emails and sticky notes: late parcels are discovered too late, lost boxes have no trace, and nobody can open the right carrier page without hunting for the number.

## The solution

Register the tracking number of every inbound parcel and one click to the carrier page, lost ones included.

## Main features

- A register of inbound parcels with tracking number and carrier.
- One-click carrier tracking link computed from the tracking number.
- Expected and received dates with a stored late flag.
- States for draft, in transit, received and lost.
- Received date stamped when the parcel arrives.
- Lost parcels kept with their reason in the chatter.
- Late parcels grouped for follow-up in one screen.
- Multi-company isolation out of the box.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Parcel Watch** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 18.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 19.0 and 20.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
