# OfficeStock

No More Emergency Shopping

## The problem

Office consumables run out without warning: quantities are guessed, restocks are rushed and the same items are bought twice while others are missing.

## The solution

Keep the small consumables of every office ordered in time without the endless emergency shopping trips.

## Main features

- A register of office supplies with location, on-hand quantity and minimum.
- Restock requests with requested quantity, request date and order date.
- The shortage computed and stored on every supply item.
- Pending and ordered states tracked on every restock request.
- One restock-needed screen for items below their minimum level.
- A negative on-hand quantity is refused for you.
- A negative requested quantity is refused for you.
- Multi-company isolation out of the box.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Office Stock** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 20.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 18.0 and 19.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
