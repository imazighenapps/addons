# LeaseBook

Leases Run Themselves

## The problem

Lease payment schedules live in contract PDFs and spreadsheets, so monthly amounts are copied by hand, payments are forgotten, and indexations or variances surface only months later.

## The solution

Keep the payment schedule of every lease and generate the monthly entry without the spreadsheet errors.

## Main features

- A register of leases with partner, term and monthly amount.
- Automatic generation of a dated payment schedule for the term.
- Posted and remaining amounts computed and stored on the lease.
- One-click posting of each monthly schedule line.
- Running and closed states with a controlled workflow.
- Guards that refuse duplicate generation or impossible dates.
- Schedule lines protected once the lease is closed.
- Multi-company isolation out of the box.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Lease Book** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 19.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 18.0 and 20.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
