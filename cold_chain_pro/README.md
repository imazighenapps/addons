# ColdChain

Every Cold Point Under Watch

## The problem

The HACCP plan lives on a laminated sheet next to each cold room, temperature checks are written in pencil and transcribed late, and when an auditor asks for two years of readings nobody can produce them with the corrections that were made.

## The solution

Register every cold point of your HACCP plan, log its temperature readings, and let each out-of-range reading be flagged, corrected and traced with a written explanation.

## Main features

- Cold points with a defined safe temperature range and a check frequency.
- Temperature readings with operator, timestamp and instant range check.
- Out-of-range readings gathered in one breach screen.
- A correction that stamps who fixed the breach and why.
- Live counters of readings and open breaches on each point.
- Archived cold points are kept out of daily use without losing history.
- Multi-company isolation out of the box.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Cold Chain** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 18.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 19.0 and 20.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
