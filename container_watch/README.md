# ContainerWatch

Containers Without Surprises

## The problem

Container ETAs change, charges pile up in emails and nobody knows who owes what before the truck reaches the gate; delays and unpaid fees are discovered after the fact.

## The solution

Follow your import containers from booking to warehouse with the ETA, the charges and who owes what.

## Main features

- One record per container with booking, vessel and route.
- Planned and actual arrival dates with a stored delay flag.
- Charges with type, amount and the party who owes them.
- Totals per container for a clear landed cost view.
- States from booked to at sea, arrived and released.
- Actual arrival stamped when the container is released.
- Unsettled charges visible at a glance.
- Multi-company isolation out of the box.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Container Watch** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 18.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 19.0 and 20.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
