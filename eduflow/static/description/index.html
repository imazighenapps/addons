📋 Admissions & Enrollments

  • Public online pre-registration form, published on your website — no login required for prospective families.
  • Kanban pipeline: New Application → Under Review → Interview / Test → Accepted / Refused → Enrolled.
  • Required-document checklist per level (birth certificate, previous report card, medical file...), with an option to block acceptance until the file is complete.
  • One-click transformation of an accepted application into a confirmed enrollment, with automatic class-change history.

🏫 Classes, Timetable, Attendance & Grades

  • Levels, classes, subjects, coefficients and multi-year academic programs.
  • Timetable displayed as a real weekly calendar, not just a flat list.
  • "Take Attendance" wizard: pick a class and a date, the roster is pre-filled as Present — just correct the exceptions and validate once for the whole class. Guardians are notified automatically.
  • Grade entry grid auto-populated with every enrolled student; exams are locked for teachers once validated by Administration.
  • Automatic overall averages, ranking, and printable PDF report cards, publishable straight to the parent portal.

💳 Fees, Invoicing & Payments

  • Configurable fee types (registration, tuition, transport...) per level, year and student profile, with their own revenue account, sales journal and taxes.
  • Generate a genuine Odoo customer invoice from any fee in one click — billed to the student's financial guardian.
  • Confirming a payment automatically creates and reconciles the matching accounting payment; cancelling it un-reconciles cleanly. Works the other way round too: any payment collected from the standard Odoo "Pay Now" invoice portal flow is reflected back on the fee automatically.
  • Automatic, non-duplicated fee reminders sent to guardians at configurable milestones (e.g. 7 days before, on the due date, 7 days after).

🌐 Parent & Teacher Portal

  • Parents see, per child: profile, weekly timetable, attendance history, published report cards (PDF) and every fee with its payment status — plus a direct "Pay Online" link on invoiced, unpaid fees.
  • Teachers get a dedicated space with their weekly timetable and their classes, one click away from the backend.
  • Strict record-level security: a parent only ever sees their own children's data; a teacher only their own classes.

🔐 Security, Multi-Company & Dashboard

  • Six built-in profiles: Administrator, Management, Administration Staff, Teacher, Accountant, Parent — each with the exact access level they need.
  • Full multi-company data separation on every transactional model: students, enrollments, exams, grades, attendance, fees, payments, timetable and more.
  • Live management dashboard (headcount, absenteeism, average grade, invoiced / collected / outstanding amounts) plus a historized daily snapshot to chart KPI trends over time.
  • Full activity log (chatter) on sensitive records: exams, grades, report cards, board decisions.
