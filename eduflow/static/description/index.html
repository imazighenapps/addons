Everything a school admin office needs

Admissions & Enrollments

  • Public online pre-registration form, published on your website — no login required for prospective families.
  • Kanban pipeline: New Application → Under Review → Interview / Test → Accepted / Refused → Enrolled.
  • Required-document checklist per level, including birth certificate, previous report card, medical file and other configurable documents.
  • Option to block acceptance until the required student file is complete.
  • One-click transformation of an accepted application into a confirmed enrollment, with automatic class-change history.

Classes, Timetable, Attendance & Grades

  • Levels, classes, subjects, coefficients and multi-year academic programs.
  • Timetable displayed as a real weekly calendar instead of a flat list.
  • Take Attendance wizard: select a class and date, start with the roster pre-filled as Present, correct exceptions and validate the whole class.
  • Guardians can be notified automatically when attendance is recorded.
  • Grade entry grid automatically populated with every enrolled student.
  • Exams can be locked for teachers once validated by Administration.
  • Automatic overall averages, ranking and printable PDF report cards.
  • Report cards can be published directly to the parent portal.

Fees, Invoicing & Payments

  • Configurable fee types such as registration, tuition and transport per level, year and student profile.
  • Each fee type can use its own revenue account, sales journal and taxes.
  • Generate a genuine Odoo customer invoice from any fee in one click, billed to the student's financial guardian.
  • Confirming a payment automatically creates and reconciles the matching accounting payment.
  • Cancelling a payment cleanly removes the reconciliation.
  • Standard Odoo "Pay Now" invoice payments are reflected back on the corresponding school fee.
  • Automatic fee reminders can be sent to guardians at configurable milestones.

Parent & Teacher Portal

  • Parents see each child's profile, weekly timetable, attendance history, published report cards and school fees.
  • Parents can see the payment status of every fee.
  • Invoiced and unpaid fees can provide a direct "Pay Online" link.
  • Teachers get a dedicated portal space with their weekly timetable and their assigned classes.
  • Strict record-level security ensures parents only see their own children's data.
  • Teachers only access their own classes and relevant records.

Security, Multi-Company & Dashboard

  • Six built-in profiles: Administrator, Management, Administration Staff, Teacher, Accountant and Parent.
  • Each profile receives the exact access level required for its role.
  • Full multi-company data separation across students, enrollments, exams, grades, attendance, fees, payments, timetable and more.
  • Live management dashboard with headcount, absenteeism, average grade, invoiced, collected and outstanding amounts.
  • Historized daily KPI snapshots allow management to track trends over time.
  • Full chatter activity logs on sensitive records such as exams, grades, report cards and board decisions.

Clean, modular installation

The base EduFlow app only depends on standard Odoo modules: base, mail, portal, account, hr and website.
