# LateFee

Late Costs What It Should

## The problem

Statutory late payment interest is owed but rarely claimed because the legal rate, the days late and the debit amount are computed by hand long after the invoice became overdue.

## The solution

Compute the statutory late payment interest on overdue invoices at the legal rate and issue the debit lines.

## Main features

- Interest rules with an annual statutory rate and optional fixed fee.
- Overdue invoice lines with partner, due date and amount due.
- Days late and interest amount computed and stored for you.
- Draft and issued states with a clear claim workflow.
- Totals per rule to see how much interest has been issued.
- Guards that refuse negative amounts or impossible dates.
- Manual recalculation action to refresh the computed interest.
- Multi-company isolation out of the box.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Late Fee** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 18.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 19.0 and 20.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
