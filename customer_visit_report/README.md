# VisitReport

Customer Visit and Field Reports

## The problem

Customer visit outcomes captured in unstructured notes are hard to report, share and follow up consistently across the sales team.

## The solution

Record structured customer visit reports with purpose, summary, outcomes and follow-up actions, track them through draft/submitted/reviewed states, and report by customer and rep.

## Main features

- Structured visit reports linked to customer and responsible user.
- Purpose, summary, outcomes and follow-up action fields.
- Next-action date and follow-up needed flag.
- Draft, submitted and reviewed workflow states.
- Search and group by customer, responsible and period.
- Chatter tracking on reports.
- Multi-company safe, no Enterprise dependency.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Visit Reports** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 19.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 18.0 and 20.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
