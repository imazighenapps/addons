# JobCostPro - Job Costing, Committed Costs and Real Margin

## The problem

A job is quoted with a comfortable margin, then extra orders, change requests and late subcontractor invoices pile up outside the budget. Actual and committed costs are never compared with the budget on the same screen, so the overrun is discovered when the job is closed and the margin is already gone.

## The solution

Compare the budget of every job with its actual and committed costs, cap the progress at 100 percent and flag the job before an overrun destroys the margin.

## Main features

- Budget revenue and budget cost per job.
- Cost lines typed as labor, material, subcontract, overhead or other.
- Committed flag for costs engaged but not yet invoiced.
- Actual cost and committed cost kept strictly separate.
- Margin and margin rate including the commitments.
- Progress on the budget cost, bounded between 0 and 100 percent.
- Overrun alert on actual plus committed cost, above a threshold.
- Draft, running and closed states, with budget and lines locked.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Job Costing** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 20.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 18.0 and 19.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
