# PolicyAck

Employee Policy Acknowledgement Tracking

## The problem

Employee policy acknowledgements tracked manually lack a central, auditable record per policy and employee.

## The solution

Maintain a register of company policies and track employee acknowledgements with status and date, providing an auditable per-policy, per-employee trail.

## Main features

- Policy register with version and effective date.
- Acknowledgement records linking employee and policy.
- Pending and acknowledged states with acknowledgement date.
- Actions to acknowledge and reset records.
- Search and group by policy, employee and state.
- Chatter tracking on records.
- Multi-company safe, no Enterprise dependency.

## Installation

1. Install the module from the Apps list.
2. Open the **Policy Acknowledgement** menu.
3. Create your first record and follow the guided empty state.

## Compatibility

- Odoo 20.0 Community and Enterprise.
- Odoo 18.0 and 19.0 versions are provided separately.
- No external Python dependency.
